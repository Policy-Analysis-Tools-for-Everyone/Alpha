# M01: Philippine film industry

**From** [Chair, House Creative Industries Committee] **to**
[Chair, FDCP]. Raw: [memo](../../raw/m01-philippine-film-industry.md),
[feedback](../../raw/m01-feedback.md).

## In a minute

Filipino films earn a third of what they did in 2019, because tickets cost too
much against wages and audiences have moved to streaming. The memo tells the
film council to move its location-rebate money into a fund that sells Filipino
films abroad, starting with the 10.8 million overseas Filipinos, and to co-fund
an Oscars campaign.

## Checks

- **Evidence living in the cover note.** The 2019 to 2024 box office fall
  ($144.5M to $45.5M), ticket prices against the minimum wage, the 67%
  streaming share and 3 profitable films out of 40 are all in the cover note.
  The FDCP chair, the memo's reader, never sees them.
- **"Realigning a third of the agency's budget typically allotted for film
  location incentives ($1.53M in FY2026)".** Location incentives are 31% of
  the $5.1M FY2026 budget, $1.58M. So $1.53M is the whole location line (30%),
  roughly a third of the agency's budget, not a third of the location line. A
  reader has to work that out.
- **The Oscars campaign** at $3M is 59% of the FY2026 proposed budget. The
  memo says *co-fund* but gives no share.
- **The tourism department** is asked for 0.28% of $53.2M, about $149,000.
- **Terms.** "Best International Feature Film" in the table, "Best Foreign
  Language Film" in the recommendation. The category was renamed in 2020.

## Moves worth keeping

- **Stakeholder reaction by option.** One cell per actor per option says who
  supports what. A marker singled it out.
- **Name the loser and protect them.** Cost-sharing with producers "so as not
  to lock-in its funding at the expense of other programs benefiting IGA."
- **Move a programme rather than cut it.** Location rebates survive by moving
  to a partner with a reason to fund them (tourism).
- **A register matched to the reader.** A film-maker writing to a film-maker,
  in film headings. Contested: see `../tensions.md` T4.
