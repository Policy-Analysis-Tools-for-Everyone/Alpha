# M05: UK clean energy workforce

**From** [author] for The Green Jobs Foundation **to** [Secretary
of State for Energy Security and Net Zero]. Raw:
[memo](../../raw/m05-uk-clean-energy-workforce.md),
[feedback](../../raw/m05-feedback.md).

## In a minute

The UK is on course to be more than 200,000 green-skilled energy workers short
of what its 2030 clean power mission needs, because women and marginalised
groups are shut out, employers have cut training, and recruitment is
fragmented. DESNZ should commit to a 20-year skills plan, scale London's
integrated employment service, and attach skills conditions to clean energy
funding.

## Checks

- **47.7% of 571,000** is 272,367. Checks.
- **"More than 300,000 new people"** against a 650,000-job commitment and
  272,400 existing clean-energy roles. 650,000 less 272,400 is 377,600, so the
  300,000 needs a source or a step it doesn't show.
- **The deficit's source is older than the target it's applied to.** The
  200,000 figure is PwC 2022; the 650,000-job commitment it is measured
  against came later.
- **A projection whose date had passed.** "Projected to occupy less than 25% of
  green jobs by 2025", in a memo written in 2025, from a retailer's press
  release about tradespeople (reference 7).
- **Reference 13** (No Wrong Door) is listed and never cited, at the one point
  in Option 2 that relies on it.

## Moves worth keeping

- **Take the reader's own criteria and add one.** The government's 4 mission
  tests, plus a "just" supply of workers.
- **Two options when the choice is binary**: treat the symptoms or build the
  system.
- **Each driver ends in its consequence for the mission.**
- **Prototype first.** Test the London model in a few small regions before
  scaling.
- **Signpost.** A marker credited it with telling the reader where it is going.
