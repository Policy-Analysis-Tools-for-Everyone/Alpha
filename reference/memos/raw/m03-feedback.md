## Credited

- Detailed and well informed.
- A clear, credible set of options.
- Feasibility handled well, especially by tying the recommendation to a new mayor's political capital.
- One marker thought the recommendation of Option I was the right call.

## Pushed on

- **Opponents.** The memo engages supporters well but not those opposed, including those who appear supportive and are not.
- **A second-order risk.** If the policy works, homeless people from elsewhere may move to Somerville.
- **Politics of the option.** Housing people for free in a housing crisis will be politically hard.
- **I or IV?** One marker thought the reasoning pointed to Option IV as the primary recommendation. The other agreed with Option I, but wanted the spillover benefits of housing (health, rehabilitation, employment) made stronger, because they decide how the long-term risks of I and IV compare.
- **Hope against evidence.** Where something is hoped for, replace it with evidence or a way to gather it.

## Defence and peer critique

- A confident defence that covered costs, locations and stakeholder incentives, and acknowledged trade-offs. Stigmatisation came up well in discussion; a line in the memo saying why it was left out, or a discarded option, would have helped.
