## Credited

- The cover note sets out the authorising environment: the government's balance between business and consumers, and the gap between the industry's public announcements and its private worries.
- A precise, quantified problem definition.
- Framing the issue against both the growth mission and environmental goals.
- The analysis of the two existing instruments diagnoses a structural mismatch: one supports supply, neither supports demand for UK recycled plastic.
- Good use of evidence, both for the problem and for the recommendation's feasibility.
- Tying the recommendation to an existing revenue stream makes it feasible.

## Pushed on

- **Criteria** are implied in the analysis, not stated as their own section, which weakens the link from problem to options.
- **Implementation** is thin. Setting up a working group defers the work rather than doing it.
- **Stakeholders.** It is not clear how the recommendation meets the concerns raised for the public and for local authorities.

## Defence and peer critique

- Strong presentation that added context and tied the core argument back to the stakeholders.
- Peer questions pushed others to the core of their problem and recommendation.
