## Credited

- Deep knowledge of the sector, evident in the memo and the defence.
- Sharp writing.
- The stakeholder reaction table: it shows concretely how each actor would respond to each option.

## Pushed on

- **Why film?** The memo assumes film deserves the money more than other art forms, such as novels or visual arts. It neither argues the case nor benchmarks film against them, and doesn't ask whether those sectors would object.
- **Density.** The memo tries to cover problem, stakeholders, implementation, budget and risk in a short space, which dilutes the core argument. A narrower memo with the rest in supporting detail might have been stronger.

## Defence and peer critique

- Answers were well informed but too long; tighter answers would have carried more authority.
- Suggesting consultants for implementation sat awkwardly with the aim of keeping the budget tight.
- In critiquing M04, a good push for comparators (what are the innovation leaders elsewhere doing?), but some questions were definitional rather than probing the proposal's logic or feasibility.
