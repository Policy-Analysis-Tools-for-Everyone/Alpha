## Credited

- Good structure and a clear problem.
- Well written, with evident experience of the field.
- The stakeholder mapping shows the political economy.
- Criteria framed as questions, which makes them usable.

## Pushed on

- **No numbers.** The memo needs a cost-benefit view, even rough estimates. The authoriser may not need them today, but will be asked.
- **Proportion.** A lot of space explains the crisis and the policy context to a reader who works in it daily. That reader needs the how. The implementation section is 3 short bullets against a long set-up.
- **If context is given,** it should explain the industry's fundamentals (why this is structural, not cyclical) more than the policies.

## Defence and peer critique

- A strong defence that was honest about job displacement and didn't oversell.
- In critiquing M01, good questions on operationalising the proposal and on whether streaming is really the root cause. A marker suggested the bigger question for M01 was why arts money should go to film over other art forms.
