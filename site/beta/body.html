Practitioners

Practitioner beta

Use policymemo.ai on a real policy problem and help shape the beta.

We invite people in small groups, so requesting access doesn't mean you'll get in straight away. We'll email to say we've received your request, and again if we can invite you.

{{practitioner_cta}}

What happens next

  1. You send a request. It takes about two minutes.
  2. We confirm we've received it. You'll get an email.
  3. We review requests and invite people in waves. Each wave is small, so we can support everyone in it and act on what they tell us.
  4. If you're invited, you get set-up instructions. Setting it up takes between 2 and 10 minutes, in Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot or Gemini.
