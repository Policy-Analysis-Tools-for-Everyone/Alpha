# Syntheses

Cross-session reviews. One author, one date, one snapshot.

Different from [`../wiki/findings.md`](../wiki/findings.md), which is the maintainer's
continuously re-mined view of everything here. A synthesis is somebody's argument at a moment;
the wiki is the current state. When they disagree, the wiki is what has been checked most
recently and the synthesis is the record of what someone concluded.

**A synthesis may rest on sessions this repository does not hold.** Say so at the top of the
file when it does, and never promote its findings to the wiki as though the transcripts were
available for checking.

**Change proposals stay proposals.** A report can recommend a skill edit. Recording it here is
not adopting it.
