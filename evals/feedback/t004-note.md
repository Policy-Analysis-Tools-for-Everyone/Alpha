> I tested Alpha in Claude first with a policy area, and then as a second test I used a policy
> memo I'd written for a previous semester.
>
> Overall, I really liked how it challenged the way I had framed the problem and helped break
> down some of the different issues I had bundled together. It also picked up on claims where
> the evidence wasn't quite enough.
>
> One thing I noticed was the stakeholder mapping. Claude missed some actors I had included,
> especially private-sector ones, but once I shared my memo, it recognised they were important.
> So it might be interesting to test whether the stakeholder analysis could be a bit more
> systematic before narrowing the problem too much.
>
> I also tried pushing it a bit by asking it to assume things that weren't supported by the
> evidence and just move ahead with a recommendation. I thought it handled that really well
> too.
>
> One thing I'd be curious to test more is how it prioritises and knows when to stop, as the
> analysis became quite comprehensive as we went along. It could be interesting to try it with
> a strict time constraint and see what it decides is actually essential to investigate.
>
> But overall, really enjoyed testing it - super useful and it definitely made me rethink parts
> of my original analysis!
