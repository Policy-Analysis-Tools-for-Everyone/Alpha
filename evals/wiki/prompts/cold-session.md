# One cold session

**Status:** the largest gap in the repository. Fifteen sessions, three testers, none cold.

## What it would settle

Whether any of this generalises. `../../README.md` says cold runs should drive the next major
revision, and there has never been one.

## Evidence in hand

Three sessions where a defect was caught only because the tester knew their own material:
`problem-1` (recurrence was established, the agent offered it as a kill condition), `problem-2`
(a user need flagged provisional by the tester's own research), `problem-3` (three claims about
a memo that named no audience).

**All three are invisible to someone working a borrowed case.** That is the argument for cold
testing and also the reason a cold session will find *different* things, not fewer.

## What is missing

Someone who has not read the skill files, has not seen `evals/`, and has not used MDEE. The
alpha cohort currently fails all three — `../../testers.md` records familiarity as constant
during this phase.

## What would make it fail

Giving them a case they do not hold. The defects above surfaced because the tester could
contradict the agent about their own service. A cold tester on a synthetic case tests fluency,
not accuracy.

Debriefing them into the finding. The five questions exist so testers do not have to become
evaluators.
