# Matched pair: what the vague-term challenge fires on

**Status:** ready, cheap, and the evidence currently contradicts itself.

## What it would settle

F5. Whether the challenge keys on the *kind of vagueness* or on the *active capability*.

## Evidence in hand

- Fires three times on words doing several jobs — "integration", "universal", "level"
  (`problem-1`).
- Does not fire on "better and more informed decisions", with `house-rules` known loaded
  (`evidence-2`).
- Fires in full four-part form on an unquantified claim (`problem-3`), where `problem` was
  active.

The first two suggest material. The third suggests capability. Nothing separates them.

## What is missing

Two runs, same material, different active capability. Feed the same unquantified claim in a
measurement-framework context and in a problem-definition context.

## What would make it fail

Using material that is vague in both ways at once, which is most real material. The pair only
works if each input is cleanly one or the other.
